Things to Do in Laguna Brava

Discover the best things to do in Laguna Brava and explore activities offered by local communities around the lake.

Activities

The Mayan communities of the Chuj ethnicity settled around the lake offer several activities that allow visitors to enjoy Laguna Brava's natural beauty. If you're looking for exciting things to do in Laguna Brava, these guided eco-adventures are a perfect choice.

The Lake Challenge

Put your strengths to work by crossing four kilometers in a kayak guided by a local expert. This adventure is physically demanding and recommended only for visitors in excellent physical condition.

  • Duration: 5 hours approx.
  • Price: USD 50.
  • Minimum: 2 pax
