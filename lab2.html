The Local Communities

The Mayan communities of the Chuj ethnicity settled around the lake offer several activities that allow visitors to enjoy Laguna Brava's natural beauty. If you're looking for exciting things to do in Laguna Brava, these guided eco-adventures are a perfect choice. They believe that visitors and responsible ecotourism practices are essential to support the development of their communities.

The Lake Challenge

Put your strengths to work by crossing four kilometers in a kayak guided by a local expert. This adventure is physically demanding and recommended only for visitors in excellent physical condition.

In both activities, members of the Laguna Brava community provide life vests and safety guidance before you begin.

  • Duration: 5 hours approx.
  • Price: USD 50.
  • Minimum: 2 pax
